Pump Catalogue

20 industrial pump types in five families. Descriptions are general: they explain how each type works and where it is typically used.

Showing 20 of 20 pump types

01Centrifugal Pumps

Pump type 1End-Suction

Operating principle
Liquid enters axially at the impeller eye and is thrown outward by the rotating impeller, which converts shaft energy into flow and pressure.
Typical applications
General water and process-liquid transfer, cooling circuits and plant services.
Request a quote for this pump: End-Suction

Pump type 2Split-Case

Operating principle
The casing is split along the shaft axis, allowing a double-suction impeller to handle large flows and simplifying internal access.
Typical applications
Water supply and distribution, large circulation and cooling duties, utility services.
Request a quote for this pump: Split-Case

Pump type 3Vertical Inline

Operating principle
Suction and discharge connections sit on the same line, with the motor mounted vertically above the casing for a compact footprint.
Typical applications
Building services, HVAC and process circulation, pressure boosting in confined spaces.
Request a quote for this pump: Vertical Inline

Pump type 4Multistage

Operating principle
Several impellers are arranged in series on one shaft, and each stage adds pressure to the same flow.
Typical applications
Pressure boosting, boiler feed, membrane-system feed and other high-head duties.
Request a quote for this pump: Multistage

02Rotary Positive Displacement

Pump type 5External Gear

Operating principle
Two meshing external gears carry liquid between their teeth and the housing from the inlet to the outlet, delivering a steady volume per revolution.
Typical applications
Clean, lubricating or viscous liquids such as oils and fuels, and metered transfer.
Request a quote for this pump: External Gear

Pump type 6Internal Gear

Operating principle
A rotor gear turns inside a larger internal gear, forming expanding and closing chambers that draw in and push out the liquid smoothly.
Typical applications
Viscous products such as oils, resins, polymers, adhesives and paints.
Request a quote for this pump: Internal Gear

Pump type 7Rotary Lobe

Operating principle
Two lobed rotors turn in opposite directions without touching, moving a fixed volume per revolution with gentle product handling.
Typical applications
Hygienic and shear-sensitive products in food, beverage, cosmetic and pharmaceutical processes; viscous media.
Request a quote for this pump: Rotary Lobe

Pump type 8Twin-Screw

Operating principle
Two intermeshing screws move liquid axially through sealed cavities, giving smooth, low-pulsation flow.
Typical applications
Oil and fuel transfer, viscous liquids and other continuous process duties.
Request a quote for this pump: Twin-Screw

03Reciprocating Pumps

Pump type 9Piston

Operating principle
A piston moves back and forth in a cylinder; check valves direct the liquid so that each stroke displaces a defined volume.
Typical applications
Pressure services and controlled-volume transfer of a range of liquids.
Request a quote for this pump: Piston

Pump type 10Triplex Plunger

Operating principle
Three plungers working in sequence smooth out the pulsation of reciprocating flow while generating high pressure.
Typical applications
High-pressure cleaning and descaling, membrane-system feed, injection duties.
Request a quote for this pump: Triplex Plunger

Pump type 11Air-Operated Diaphragm

Operating principle
Compressed air drives two flexible diaphragms alternately. The pump is self-priming and can run dry without damage.
Typical applications
Chemical transfer, sludges, paints and slurries, drum and tank emptying.
Request a quote for this pump: Air-Operated Diaphragm

Pump type 12Diaphragm Metering

Operating principle
A diaphragm is driven back and forth to deliver an adjustable, repeatable volume with every stroke.
Typical applications
Chemical dosing, water treatment, pH control and additive injection.
Request a quote for this pump: Diaphragm Metering

04Axial & Vertical Pumps

Pump type 13Axial-Flow

Operating principle
A propeller-type impeller pushes liquid parallel to the shaft, moving very large volumes at low head.
Typical applications
Drainage and flood control, irrigation, and large-volume circulation.
Request a quote for this pump: Axial-Flow

Pump type 14Mixed-Flow

Operating principle
The impeller imparts both radial and axial motion, combining the high flow of axial designs with a higher head than an axial-flow pump.
Typical applications
Water supply, drainage, irrigation and cooling-water circulation.
Request a quote for this pump: Mixed-Flow

Pump type 15Vertical Turbine

Operating principle
A multistage bowl assembly sits submerged in the liquid on a long vertical column, while the motor stays above ground or above the tank.
Typical applications
Wells, sumps and tanks, cooling-water intakes and deep-pit pumping.
Request a quote for this pump: Vertical Turbine

Pump type 16Submersible

Operating principle
The pump and its motor form one sealed unit that operates fully immersed in the liquid being pumped.
Typical applications
Dewatering, drainage, sumps, and wastewater or utility pits.
Request a quote for this pump: Submersible

05Specialty Pumps

Pump type 17Progressive Cavity

Operating principle
A helical rotor turns inside a stator, moving the product along in sealed cavities with smooth, non-pulsating flow.
Typical applications
Viscous, abrasive or solids-laden media such as sludges, slurries and pastes.
Request a quote for this pump: Progressive Cavity

Pump type 18Peristaltic Hose

Operating principle
Rollers or shoes squeeze a flexible hose in turn, pushing the product forward; the liquid touches only the hose.
Typical applications
Abrasive, corrosive, viscous or shear-sensitive media, including slurries and chemicals.
Request a quote for this pump: Peristaltic Hose

Pump type 19Magnetic Drive

Operating principle
A magnetic coupling transmits torque through a containment shell, so no dynamic shaft seal is needed and leakage paths are reduced.
Typical applications
Hazardous, toxic, corrosive or high-value liquids in chemical and process plants.
Request a quote for this pump: Magnetic Drive

Pump type 20Liquid Jet Ejector

Operating principle
With no moving parts, a high-pressure motive liquid passes through a nozzle and creates a low-pressure zone that draws in and mixes the suction fluid.
Typical applications
Priming, mixing, tank agitation and transfer where motive liquid is available.
Request a quote for this pump: Liquid Jet Ejector
