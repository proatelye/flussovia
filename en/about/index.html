About FLUSSOVIA

FLUSSOVIA is an industrial pump brand. This website presents a catalogue of 20 pump types in five families commonly used in process plants.

What the catalogue covers

The catalogue brings together centrifugal pumps, rotary positive displacement pumps, reciprocating pumps, axial and vertical pumps, and specialty pumps. Each pump type is described by its operating principle and its typical applications, so you can see at a glance which technology suits which kind of duty.

  • Centrifugal Pumps
  • Rotary Positive Displacement
  • Reciprocating Pumps
  • Axial & Vertical Pumps
  • Specialty Pumps

How to use this website

Browse the catalogue by pump family, read the general description of each type, and send a quote enquiry from the contact page with your pump type and application details.

Industrial applications

The pump types in the catalogue are typically used across applications such as:

  • Water supply, circulation and cooling
  • Pressure boosting and high-head duties
  • Chemical transfer and dosing
  • Viscous products: oils, resins, polymers, adhesives and paints
  • Hygienic and shear-sensitive products in food, beverage, cosmetic and pharmaceutical processes
  • Slurries, sludges and abrasive media
  • Drainage, dewatering and wastewater
  • Wells, sumps, tanks and deep pits
